Wooden Crate

Asset · Shading · Retopology

A material and look development study focused on realistic surface detailing and weathered wood response. Working with a provided base model, I was responsible for retopology, UV editing, texturing, shader setup, lighting, and rendering in Houdini using RenderMan.
